How to search
Wait before searching
New logs take roughly 2 minutes to land in OpenSearch after the request runs. If your search returns nothing, wait a moment and try again.
Picking the right index
The Index dropdown controls which log location is queried. Pick the index that matches the application or component that emitted the log you're looking for. If you're not sure, try the most likely one first, then switch indexes and re-search.
Searching within results
Once results are returned, use the Find box at the top of the results to filter visible lines. Press Enter to jump to the next match, Shift+Enter for the previous, or use the Prev / Next buttons. The counter shows your current position out of total matches.
Mark, navigate, and copy lines
Select text inside any log line and click ★ Mark selection (or press Ctrl+M / Cmd+M) to mark every line touched by the selection. Marked lines get a gold left border.

Use the Prev / Next buttons in the marked toolbar to step through them, or click the small × on a marked line to unmark just that one. Clear removes all marks.

Copy marked copies just the marked lines to your clipboard. Copy all copies every visible line. Use Expand all / Collapse all to show or hide the prefix details (thread, full class name) for parsed lines.
